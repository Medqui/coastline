# Coastline PMS user manual

Coastline PMS helps a hotel team manage rooms, guest stays and hotel finances in one place. Each staff member signs in with their own account. Their role and property assignment determine which screens and actions they can use.

## Who uses Coastline PMS?

| Role | Main responsibility | Typical access |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Owns the hotel workspace and reviews the business | All assigned properties, reservations, room operations, guest folios, expenses, financial reports, staff invitations, role changes and property setup. Owners can also manage suppliers, record and pay bills, correct supplier records and reconcile cash and bank balances. |
| **Manager** | Runs day-to-day hotel operations | Reservations, front desk, room operations, guest records, finance reports, expenses, suppliers and invoice entry for assigned properties. Supplier payments and corrections are reserved for owners and accountants. Managers can add a property when their account has access to all properties. Owners retain control of invitations and role changes. |
| **Front desk** | Handles bookings and guest stays | Reservations, availability, front desk, guest records, check-in, folios, deposits, guest payments, room moves and early checkout. Front desk staff can see guest folio amounts, but do not get the business finance and expense reports. |
| **Accountant** | Records and reviews hotel finances | Financial reports, department profit and loss, trial balance, expense entry and receipts, suppliers, invoices, supplier payments and corrections, reconciliation, and the guest directory. The reservation and front desk screens are not available to this role. |
| **Housekeeping** | Prepares rooms for guests | Room board and housekeeping status. Housekeeping staff can mark rooms dirty, clean or inspected. Only an owner or manager can mark a room out of order. Guest records, reservations and finance screens are not available to this role. |

**Staff do not all get the same access.** Access is limited by role and by the properties assigned to the staff member. A role change takes effect immediately. Staff should use their own sign-in; do not share accounts.

## Getting started

1. Sign in with your own account. A new owner who has not set up a hotel will be guided through organization and property setup.
2. The owner or manager sets up properties, rooms and the standard nightly room rate. Mark rooms clean or inspected before assigning them to arriving guests.
3. The owner invites each staff member from **Properties & team**, choosing a role and property. The PMS creates a secure invite link that expires after seven days. The owner must copy and send the link to the staff member. The staff member opens it, signs in or creates an account using the invited email address, and accepts the invitation.
4. Use the property selector to switch between properties your account is allowed to access.

## The normal guest workflow

### 1. Check availability and make a reservation

1. Open **Reservations**, then select **Availability calendar**.
2. Choose the start date and calendar range. Move through dates with the arrow buttons.
3. Select an available room night, or choose **New reservation**.
4. Enter the guest's name, contact details, arrival and departure dates, room, number of adults and any useful notes. Review the price for each night and the quoted room total.
5. Confirm the reservation. The reservation appears as confirmed. The PMS checks that the room is available for those dates when the reservation is saved.

For a guest who arrives without a prior booking, use **Front desk → Walk-in booking**. This creates a same-day reservation. After saving it, check the guest in using the arrival list.

Owners and managers may choose **Approve a fixed nightly rate** for a discount or complimentary stay while creating a reservation. A reason of 5–500 characters is required. Each standard nightly rate, the approved rate and the approving user are recorded. Front-desk staff book the scheduled standard prices. A zero scheduled price also requires managerial approval. Complimentary room nights create no revenue charge; guest extras are still payable. If rates change while the form is open, review the refreshed quote before confirming.

A booking is a room hold and price quote. It does not count as accounting revenue until the room charge is posted.

### 2. Check in the guest

1. Open **Front desk** and find the guest in today's arrivals.
2. Confirm the guest and room details. The room must be clean or inspected, and check-in is allowed on the property's local arrival date.
3. Select **Check in**. The guest will appear as in-house.

If the guest has paid an advance, open **Reservations**, select **Deposit**, and record the amount, payment method and reference. Deposits are tracked separately until applied to the guest's charges.

### 3. Manage the stay and folio

Open the guest's folio from **Front desk** or **Reservations** to:

- Post completed room nights. Each room night can only be posted once.
- Add an extra charge such as food or laundry, choosing its department.
- Record a full or partial payment, its payment method and reference.
- Apply an advance deposit to the guest's balance.
- Print the folio.

Review every amount before saving. Posted accounting entries are not ordinary editable notes. Ask the owner or accountant how to correct a mistake.

### 4. Move a guest or check out early

- To move an in-house guest, open the room board and select a clean or inspected destination room. The move is recorded in the reservation history.
- If a guest leaves early, use **Early checkout** and enter the actual departure date. The room will need cleaning before it can be sold again.

### 5. Check out on the planned departure date

1. Open the in-house guest's folio.
2. Post any due room nights and remaining charges. Record payments and apply or refund any remaining advance.
3. Check out is available on or after the planned departure date, once the folio balance and unapplied deposits are both zero.
4. After checkout, housekeeping should clean and inspect the room before its next stay.

If a confirmed guest will not arrive, use **Cancel / no-show** and enter a reason. A no-show can only be recorded on or after the arrival date.

## Room and housekeeping workflow

Open **Rooms & housekeeping** to see rooms grouped by their operating state. Update a room after cleaning or inspection. A room's housekeeping state is separate from its reservation state: a clean room may already be reserved for a future date. An owner or manager should mark a room out of order when it cannot be sold.

### Maintenance work orders

Use the maintenance section on **Rooms & housekeeping** to report a repair with its room, title, details and priority. Front desk and housekeeping can report issues. Owners and managers can also block a room from sale, start work, resolve a task or cancel it. Closing a task requires work notes. Closed tasks remain in the history.

Move or check out an in-house guest before blocking their room. A maintenance block prevents new bookings, check-ins and room moves into the room. Review any existing future reservations and arrange another room before arrival. Closing all blocking tasks releases the room; housekeeping status remains separate and still needs to be clean or inspected for check-in.

### Manage room rates

Owners and managers manage prices in the **Room rates** section of **Rooms & housekeeping**. Change a room type's base rate with a reason, or schedule a named rate with its first and last charged nights. The last night is included; the departure date is excluded from the guest's quote. Scheduled periods for the same room type cannot overlap. Outside a scheduled period, the base rate applies.

To replace a scheduled period, stop using it with a reason and create the replacement. Existing bookings keep the exact nightly prices agreed when they were created, even after a base rate changes or a period is retired. Room moves keep those agreed prices. The guest folio can show the nightly quote; posted charges remain a separate section.

## Expenses and financial reports

### Record a paid expense

1. Open **Finance & reports** and enter the expense description, category, department, amount, payment method and any vendor details.
2. Attach a JPG, PNG or PDF receipt if available.
3. Review the entry and save it. The expense is recorded in the hotel's books.

### Record an unpaid supplier invoice

1. Open **Finance & reports → Supplier bills & payments** with the balances date set to today.
2. Add the supplier's name and contact details, or choose an existing active supplier. Use **Supplier directory → Edit details** to update or deactivate a supplier.
3. Enter the supplier invoice number, invoice date, accounting date, due date, description, category, department and invoice amount. The accounting date controls when the expense appears in reports and must be on or after the invoice date.
4. Attach an invoice or receipt if available (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB), then select **Record unpaid bill**. The expense and amount owed are recorded together.
5. If the attachment fails, find the existing bill and use **Attach invoice receipt** to retry. Do not create another bill. **Replace invoice receipt** changes the attachment while preserving the posted financial facts.

Do not enter the same invoice as both a paid expense and an unpaid bill. The invoice number is unique for an active bill from that supplier.

### Record a supplier payment

An owner or accountant finds the unpaid bill and opens **Record payment**. Enter the actual amount paid, payment date, method and bank/cash reference. Partial payments are supported. The payment reduces the amount owed and cash/bank balance; it does not record the expense again. Recording a payment in the PMS does not send money to a supplier.

The PMS rejects payments above the unpaid balance, including backdated payments that would overpay an earlier period. Managers may record invoices and view balances but cannot pay or reverse them. Front desk and housekeeping have no access to supplier accounts.

### Correct a supplier record

Owners and accountants can use **Correct payment record** to reverse an incorrect payment, with a date and reason. Verify what actually happened in the bank and supplier records first. The original payment stays in the history, with a linked reversal.

To cancel an incorrect invoice, first reverse any recorded payments, then open **Correct / void bill**. The correction date must follow the bill's recorded activity. The void reverses the expense and liability. A corrected replacement can then use the same invoice number. Posted amounts and dates cannot be edited or deleted.

### Follow up unpaid supplier bills

Change **Balances as of** to review outstanding amounts and overdue groups on a past date. Payments, reversals and voids take effect on their accounting dates. Return the view to today before entering or correcting records. Summary totals include every bill; the lists show the latest 100 bills and payments.

### Review reports

Owners, managers and accountants can review profit and loss by account and department, the cumulative trial balance, balance sheet, cash-flow statement and guest receivables aging. Refresh reports after adding or backdating a transaction. New room-night entries belong to Accommodation; staff choose the department for extras, expenses and supplier invoices. Older entries use their recorded source where possible; other historical entries are shown as unallocated.

An owner or accountant starts a cash or bank reconciliation with its period, opening balance and statement closing balance. Open **Statement**, then import a CSV with `date`, `description`, optional `reference`, signed `amount`, and optional `balance` columns. Separate `debit` and `credit` columns are also accepted. Use **Auto-match exact amounts** first, then review remaining lines manually. A flagged exception records the reason but still blocks completion until the missing or incorrect ledger entry is posted and matched. Incorrect matches can be removed with a required correction reason.

The reports use posted accounting activity. Room charges recognize revenue when posted; applying an advance settles the guest balance. Receiving an advance deposit creates a liability. Unpaid supplier bills recognize an expense on the accounting date; paying them later settles the liability. Payments collected and recognized income are different figures.

## Tips and common questions

- **A screen is missing:** your role or property assignment may not include it. Ask the hotel owner to confirm your access.
- **No rooms appear for your dates:** check the property, dates, room status and existing reservations. Out-of-order rooms, maintenance blocks and overlapping bookings cannot be selected.
- **A reservation cannot be checked in:** confirm the arrival date is today in the hotel's local time and the room is clean or inspected.
- **A report is empty:** check its date range. Reports include posted income and expenses, not booking quotes or unposted room nights.
- **A page says that the latest database update is required:** contact the hotel administrator before retrying the action.
- **Invite link expired:** ask the owner to create a new invitation.

## Current product limits

- Each reservation covers one room. To reserve multiple rooms, make a separate reservation for each room.
- Team invitations are shared manually by the owner; the PMS does not send invitation emails.
- Taxes and levies are not currently included in the booking workflow. Confirm and calculate any applicable taxes outside the PMS until that feature is added.
- The PMS does not yet include a restaurant/bar POS, sales-item catalogue, purchase orders, stock tracking, cashier shift close, or payroll. Extra guest charges can be entered manually on a folio.
- Financial reports include historical profit and loss, department reporting, trial balance, balance sheet, cash flow, guest receivables aging and dated supplier payable aging after migrations 011–014.
- Bank statements are uploaded as CSV files; direct bank feeds and many-to-one settlement matching are not available.
- Database and receipt backups must be configured by the hotel administrator in Supabase.
